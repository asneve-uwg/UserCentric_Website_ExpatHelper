Malaysia overview

High‑level snapshot for newcomers: language, major cities, typical costs, and common visa pathways.

  • Capital Kuala Lumpur
  • Languages Malay (Bahasa Malaysia), English widely understood
  • Currency Malaysian Ringgit (MYR)

Visa & Documents

Work (Employment Pass)

  • Job offer from a Malaysian employer
  • Passport with at least 6 months validity
  • Degree/qualification proofs
  • Company sponsorship letters

Student Pass

  • Offer letter from an educational institution
  • Financial proof for tuition/living
  • Medical screening certificate

Housing

  • Popular areas: KLCC, Bangsar, Mont Kiara
  • Check tenancy terms: deposit, notice period, utilities
  • Use verified platforms and request proof of ownership

Healthcare

  • Register with local clinics; carry passport/ID
  • Consider private insurance for broader coverage

Money & Taxes

  • Open a bank account with proof of address and passport
  • Understand tax residency rules based on days present

Safety & Fraud

  • Use escrow or verified platforms for rentals
  • Beware of requests to pay via gift cards or crypto
